# Quarterly Financial Report - Q1 2024

## Executive Summary

This report presents the financial performance for Q1 2024. Overall revenue increased by 18% compared to the same period last year. The North region showed the strongest growth with a 22% increase.

## Revenue Analysis

### Total Revenue

Total revenue for Q1 2024 reached $4.96 million, compared to $4.2 million in Q1 2023. This represents a year-over-year growth of 18%.

### Regional Breakdown

- North Region: $2.61 million (22% growth)
- South Region: $2.35 million (14% growth)

### Product Performance

Widget A continues to be the top performer with $1.79 million in revenue. Widget B generated $1.32 million in revenue.

## Key Findings

1. The North region outperformed expectations by 8%
2. Widget A sales increased 25% in the North region
3. Customer acquisition cost decreased by 12%
4. Customer retention rate improved to 94%

## Recommendations

1. Increase marketing spend in the South region to capture growth potential
2. Launch Widget C in Q2 to diversify product offerings
3. Invest in automation to maintain profit margins

## Conclusion

Q1 2024 demonstrated strong financial performance across all metrics. The company is well-positioned for continued growth in the remaining quarters.
