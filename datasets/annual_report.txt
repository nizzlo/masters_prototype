# Meridian Group plc — Annual Report & Accounts FY2023

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Meridian Group plc delivered another year of strong financial performance in FY2023, achieving record revenues of **$8.74 billion**, representing growth of **14.2%** versus FY2022 ($7.65 billion). Our three operating segments — Technology, Healthcare, and Consumer Goods — all grew year-on-year, with Technology outperforming expectations. Net income reached **$1.60 billion**, yielding a **net profit margin of 18.3%**, up from 17.1% in the prior year.

Group EBITDA grew to **$2.14 billion** (EBITDA margin: 24.5%), driven by disciplined cost management and operational leverage across the Technology segment. Basic **earnings per share (EPS) was $2.84**, compared to $2.41 in FY2022. The Board is pleased to declare a final **dividend of $0.48 per share** (FY2022: $0.42), reflecting confidence in the Group's long-term earnings capacity.

Chief Executive **Charles Whitmore** commented: "FY2023 has demonstrated the strength of our diversified business model. The strategic investments we made in cloud infrastructure and data analytics capabilities are now delivering measurable returns."

## 3. Technology Segment Review

The Technology segment continued to be Meridian's growth engine, generating **revenue of $3.82 billion** in FY2023, a year-on-year increase of **22.1%** (FY2022: $3.13 billion). The segment benefited from strong demand for cloud migration services and enterprise software licensing.

**Technology Segment EBITDA was $893 million**, representing a margin of 23.4% (FY2022: $658 million, 21.0%). The improvement in margin reflects the shift towards higher-margin recurring software revenues, which now represent 62% of segment revenue (FY2022: 54%).

Key Technology segment achievements in FY2023:
- Secured 14 large enterprise contracts with average annual contract value exceeding $40 million
- Cloud services revenue grew 38% to $1.42 billion
- Headcount expanded by 1,840 to reach 18,200 globally
- Launched DataPilot AI platform, generating $120 million in first-year revenue
- Acquired NexGen Analytics for $340 million (completed July 2023)

The Technology segment accounted for 43.7% of Group revenue and 41.7% of Group EBITDA.

---

## 4. Healthcare Segment Review

The Healthcare segment delivered steady growth with **revenue of $2.91 billion** (FY2022: $2.60 billion), representing **growth of 11.8%**. Revenue was driven by strong performance in diagnostic imaging equipment and pharmaceutical supply chain services.

**Healthcare segment EBITDA was $698 million** (margin: 24.0%), compared to $618 million (23.8%) in FY2022. Margin improvement was driven by pricing power in diagnostic equipment and operating efficiency gains from the segment's operational excellence programme.

Key Healthcare segment highlights:
- Regulatory approval received for three new diagnostic products in the EU and US markets
- Pharmaceutical supply chain revenue grew 18% to $980 million
- Signed a 5-year exclusive supply agreement with MedGroup International, estimated value $1.8 billion
- Initiated clinical trials for BioScan Pro platform (results expected Q3 FY2024)
- Divested non-core rehabilitation services division for $145 million (August 2023)

The Healthcare segment represented 33.3% of Group revenue.

---

## 5. Consumer Goods Segment Review

The Consumer Goods segment faced a more challenging trading environment due to persistent consumer price sensitivity and elevated input costs. Revenue was **$2.01 billion** (FY2022: $1.91 billion), representing **growth of 5.4%**. Organic growth was 3.2%; the remaining 2.2% came from the bolt-on acquisition of NaturalBrew Ltd (completed February 2023).

**Consumer Goods segment EBITDA was $241 million** (margin: 11.9%), down from 13.1% in FY2022, reflecting higher raw material costs and investment in brand repositioning.

Key Consumer Goods actions taken in FY2023:
- Launched premium product tier across five core categories, contributing $180 million in incremental revenue
- Restructured European distribution network, generating annualised savings of $28 million
- Reduced packaging material costs by 9% through supply chain renegotiation
- E-commerce channel grew to 31% of Consumer Goods revenue (FY2022: 24%)

## 6. Balance Sheet and Cash Flow

The Group maintained a **strong balance sheet** throughout FY2023. Total assets at year-end were $14.2 billion (FY2022: $12.7 billion). The increase reflects the NexGen Analytics acquisition ($340 million goodwill) and continued capital investment.

**Operating cash flow was $1.95 billion** (FY2022: $1.68 billion), reflecting strong conversion of profits into cash. Capital expenditure totalled **$620 million** (FY2022: $580 million), of which 58% was invested in Technology infrastructure. Free cash flow of **$1.33 billion** was used to fund the dividend ($270 million), acquisitions ($340 million for NexGen), and debt repayment ($180 million).

Net debt closed the year at **$1.5 billion** (0.7x EBITDA), down from $1.8 billion at FY2022 year-end. The Group has a $1.5 billion revolving credit facility, undrawn at year-end.

Chief Financial Officer **Rebecca Sung** noted: "Our cash generation remains a key competitive advantage, enabling us to invest for growth while maintaining a strong balance sheet and increasing returns to shareholders."

## 7. Risk Factors

The Board has identified the following as principal risks for the Group:

**Macroeconomic and market risk.** Sustained inflationary pressures, rising interest rates, and slowing global economic growth could reduce enterprise technology spend and consumer purchasing power. The Group actively monitors demand indicators and maintains pricing flexibility.

**Cybersecurity and data protection risk.** The Group processes significant volumes of sensitive customer and patient data. A cyber attack or data breach could result in financial penalties, reputational damage, and operational disruption. The Board has approved a $120 million cybersecurity investment programme over FY2024–FY2025.

**Healthcare regulatory risk.** Changes to FDA, EMA, or MHRA approval processes, pricing regulations, or reimbursement policies could adversely affect Healthcare segment revenue. Three products are currently pending regulatory approval.

**Supply chain disruption risk.** Concentration of critical component supply in certain geographies creates vulnerability to geopolitical or logistical disruptions. The Group has implemented dual-sourcing for the top 50 critical components.

**Talent retention risk.** Competition for technology talent remains intense. The Group's ability to attract, develop, and retain skilled engineers, data scientists, and sales professionals is critical to the Technology segment's growth plans.

**Acquisition integration risk.** The NexGen Analytics acquisition must be successfully integrated to deliver the expected synergies of $65 million per annum by FY2026.

## 8. Corporate Governance

The Board of Meridian Group plc comprises 10 directors: the Executive Chairman **Sir Geoffrey Hartwell**, Chief Executive **Charles Whitmore**, Chief Financial Officer **Rebecca Sung**, and seven independent non-executive directors. The Board met 12 times during FY2023.

The Audit Committee, chaired by **Dame Patricia Hollis**, reviewed internal controls, financial reporting, and the external audit process. The external auditor, **PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP**, has served as Group auditor for six years. Audit fees for FY2023 were $8.4 million.

The Remuneration Committee approved an Executive Bonus Plan payout of 87% of maximum for FY2023, reflecting strong financial performance partly offset by the Consumer Goods margin decline. Long-term incentive plan (LTIP) awards vested at 92% based on three-year EPS and ROIC targets.

---

## 9. FY2024 Outlook

The Board enters FY2024 with confidence. For the full year FY2024, Meridian expects:
- Group revenue growth of **10–13%**
- EBITDA margin in the range of **24–25%**
- Capital expenditure of approximately **$680 million**
- Effective tax rate of approximately **22%**

The Technology segment is expected to continue outperforming, supported by the DataPilot AI platform and full-year contribution from NexGen Analytics. Healthcare will benefit from the new MedGroup supply agreement. Consumer Goods will focus on margin recovery through pricing actions and cost efficiency.

The Group maintains its commitment to progressive dividends and expects to grow the FY2024 dividend by at least 10%.
